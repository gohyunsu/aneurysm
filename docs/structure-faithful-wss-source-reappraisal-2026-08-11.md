This audit evaluates the proposed statement that field-accurate transient WSS
surrogates can destroy critical-flow organization. It accepts the statement as
a falsifiable application hypothesis, but separates three questions that must
not be conflated:

1. does a public asset identify stable critical structures;
2. do field-error-matched strong baselines actually disagree on them; and
3. does a minimal operator improve those endpoints without a field-accuracy
   penalty?

## 1. What the evidence supports

The strongest defensible question is narrower than a new Hodge or equivariant
architecture:

> When transient surface-WSS predictors are matched on field error, do they
> still disagree on **robust**, signed critical structures, and can a
> structure-aware operator reduce that disagreement without degrading field
> accuracy?

This could support an ISBI application contribution only if the failure
mechanism is first observed and the structural target is stable. It is not yet
a paper identity. In particular, an improvement in Cartesian MSE, SSIM, TAWSS
or OSI alone does not answer the question.

The 2026 [Aneurisk CFD record](https://zenodo.org/records/19455127) is useful but
insufficient for immediate task admission. It is an open CC BY 4.0 v1 record
with 76 selected anterior-circulation Aneurisk geometries, a 1,430,889,142-byte
VTP archive with MD5 `8c66e7bb359d04bd1a5d6db6da3f3926`, and a 1,436-byte
README. The record and README state only that the surfaces contain
OpenFOAM-derived hemodynamic and morphological fields. They do not enumerate
VTP arrays, phase count, phase alignment, coordinate correspondence, units,
critical-point labels, extraction tolerances or a case manifest.

The companion [primary paper](https://arxiv.org/html/2602.21409) identifies
fixed points and separatrices in the **cycle-averaged** WSS vector field. Its
reported trajectories are surface integral curves of that averaged field, not
critical-point worldlines across the cardiac cycle. The supplement shows
temporal evolution for one representative case, which does not establish that
all 76 released files contain a phase-resolved vector sequence. The record
summarizes zero-pressure outlets while the paper specifies a resistance
pressure condition, so exact provenance also remains unresolved without an
asset gate.

## 2. Direct-prior boundary

The proposed ingredients are already densely occupied:

- [Hodge Spectral Duality](https://arxiv.org/abs/2605.13834), accepted at ICML
  2026 with public code, already represents physical fields as discrete forms,
  splits exact/coexact/harmonic structure and learns topology-preserving
  operators on meshes;
- [SE(3)-equivariant artery-wall mesh networks](https://arxiv.org/abs/2212.05023)
  already predict directional, transient WSS over a cardiac cycle under
  varying boundary conditions;
- [RHSIA](https://arxiv.org/abs/2601.19876) already combines an aneurysm-surface
  Graph Transformer, geometric harmonic descriptors, temporal input and
  steady-flow augmentation for transient WSS surrogation;
- [trajectory-preserving time-varying vector-field compression](https://arxiv.org/abs/2510.25143)
  already targets exact preservation of critical-point trajectories;
- an aneurysm-specific [359-lesion study](https://doi.org/10.1002/cnm.3844)
  already extracts critical points at each phase, tracks them over the cardiac
  cycle and constructs critical-point influence fields;
- robust/multilevel critical-point tracking, tangent projection, conservative
  remapping and test-blind family-disjoint evaluation are direct controls or
  required hygiene.

Consequently, edge 1-forms, Hodge decomposition, equivariance, periodic time
decoding, critical-point extraction, worldline matching and a topology loss are
not individually novel. A paper would need new evidence that field-error
matching leaves a consequential structural failure, plus a minimal mechanism
that fixes it.

### Why the 31.0 leader is not repaired

The cycle-averaged target is more identifiable than cardiac-cycle worldlines
because the companion paper explicitly defines fixed points and separatrices.
However, that same paper already performs the fixed-point analysis, and Hodge,
equivariant and topology-preserving operators are direct prior. The public
manifest still does not specify the required vector array or extraction
contract. This leaves a worthwhile application experiment, but not enough
source-level residual novelty to cross 32.

The 2015 [International Aneurysm CFD Challenge](https://figshare.com/articles/dataset/Data_for_The_2015_International_Aneurysm_CFD_Challenge_/6383516)
is a useful robustness control: 28 submissions from 26 teams processed the
same five MCA aneurysm image volumes, producing large WSS variability. The
independent anatomy count is nevertheless five, not 28 or 140, so solver-team
replicates cannot be treated as patient-level evidence or a training cohort.

## 4. Architecture corrections

The submitted architecture sketch is retained only as a candidate design
space, with the following corrections.

1. **Hodge is a baseline, not the proposal by default.** HSD must be run as an
   official or faithful strong control. A custom Hodge block is unjustified
   unless a later failure analysis isolates a missing operator property.
2. **Critical points and worldlines begin as evaluation, not a loss.** Their
   coordinates are discontinuous near degenerate zeros, and assignments are
   ambiguous near birth/death or bifurcation events. A direct coordinate or
   event loss can reward matcher artifacts. Only a stable E1 target can justify
   a differentiable surrogate objective.
3. **An edge 1-form does not guarantee critical-point fidelity.** It preserves
   oriented edge integrals; zeros and Jacobians depend on the reconstruction
   map. Orientation, tangent recovery and discretization error require direct
   controls.
4. **Poincaré–Hopf bookkeeping requires a boundary contract.** Aneurysm wall
   domains have inlet/outlet boundaries. Total signed index is not a free
   invariant unless boundary zeros and boundary-vector behavior are defined.
   Absolute/relative Hodge boundary conditions must also be fixed.
5. **Eighty phases are source-specific.** They are not a biological constant.
   Phase registration, periodic closure and temporal resampling must be audited
   per asset.

If a future E1/E2 gate passes, the smallest defensible proposal is not a large
decorative stack. It is a strong, matched transient-WSS backbone with an
oriented edge-integral output and explicit tangent reconstruction, compared to
Cartesian and projected outputs. A margin-aware local `C1` or degree-control
term may be considered only after stable non-degenerate zeros are identified.
The key ablation is the representation/objective change at matched parameters,
training budget and field error; Hodge, equivariance and foundation-surface
features remain controls.

## 5. Required future evidence

A genuinely new version must proceed in order:

1. **E0 · material asset change:** an official manifest or independently
   licensed asset must expose exact surface/vector correspondence, units,
   phases, independent families and a bounded read path;
2. **E1 · method-free stability:** freeze tangent projection, remeshing,
   non-degeneracy margin, zero tolerance, temporal matching and perturbations;
3. **E2 · failure mechanism:** demonstrate structural separation among
   compute- and field-error-matched Cartesian, projected, equivariant, GHD,
   Hodge and perturbation controls;
4. **E3 · bounded development:** family-disjoint train/validation with a sealed
   outer test and frozen repair budget;
5. **E4 · confirmation:** field-error non-inferiority plus geometry-level
   improvement in signed critical-point precision/recall, total-index error,
   trajectory distance and event F1;
6. **E5 · interpretation:** reference/baseline/proposal surfaces use identical
   coordinates, phase, camera, color range and symbols, without rupture-risk or
   treatment claims.

The independent unit is patient or generator geometry family, never vertices,
triangles, phases or individual critical points. If stable structures are too
sparse, if field error determines them after matching, or if the gain
disappears against HSD/trajectory-aware controls, stop without architecture
repair.
