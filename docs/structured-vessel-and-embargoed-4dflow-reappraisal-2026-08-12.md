# Structured vessel and embargoed 4D-flow reappraisal

**Decision date:** 2026-08-12  
**Decision:** reject all six fresh candidates; open no P0, method, architecture, server query, GPU job, outer test, or paper claim.

## Executive judgment

The proposed surface-vector identity remains a scientifically meaningful *question*, not an active paper. Its central falsifiable proposition is sound: low Cartesian WSS error need not preserve signed critical points or their cardiac-cycle trajectories. The suggested edge 1-form, Hodge, equivariant, periodic, and structural-loss components are nevertheless occupied controls in the direct-prior lineage. Their combination is not novelty by itself. More importantly, the material phase-resolved surface-vector cohort and a stable method-free endpoint have not been identified. Historical job `115645.ECE-util1` therefore remains execution-incomplete, 0/10 evaluated, and scientifically undecided. It will not be repaired or rerun.

The fresh audit tested whether a more executable identity could be built from public vessel labels, RSNA multi-task segmentation, the CMRx4DFlow2026 challenge, or an open device phantom. None passes the prospective non-compensatory gate. The best additive score is 27.5/40, below 32, with an effective-independent-unit score of 0.5/5.

## What the exact sources establish

### VeNet is useful context, not a ready confirmatory benchmark

The peer-reviewed VeNet paper reports a compact vessel segmenter and a 200-mask, expert-reviewed extension of IXI-MRA. The exact public data repository inspected at head `c233ab9074f2e531028d8485794dd873e0b23b29`, however, contains 20 NIfTI masks. Its README states that the full set requires contact. Source MRAs are not redistributed in that repository, so a joined, version-pinned image–mask benchmark is not present. The paper's main experiment uses subject-level ten-fold cross-validation over 100 manually annotated IXI images and reports no separate independent test.

This does **not** invalidate the paper or imply that the other masks do not exist. It bounds what AURORA can execute reproducibly from the inspected public state. It also matters for novelty: the source itself identifies skipped-voxel topology discontinuity and topology-aware loss as future work. Generic clDice/topology-loss augmentation is therefore a direct, obvious extension rather than a defensible paper identity.

### RSNA multi-task learning crowds the obvious segmentation route

The 2026 RSNA method already combines tri-axial ROI localization with a 3D multi-task network over 4,348 series and pseudo-label/manual-correction workflows. The exact public code head is `e59e2368a722eabedc6b2228b1c6e1e7325cacd5`. The paper states a random **series-level** five-fold split. Patient-grouped separation is not explicit; this is unresolved dependence, not evidence of leakage. The work also does not provide an independent quantitative dense-reference test for all pseudo-labels. A patient-grouped revalidation could improve rigor, but rigor repair alone is not independent method novelty.

### CMRx4DFlow2026 cannot support the ISBI 2027 submission cycle

The official code repository is frozen at `f6f835f34b86464256e3ce4362e7831325f32590`. The challenge advertises more than 400 cases from more than ten centres, while the currently described regular-task partition is 138 training, 32 validation, and 43 test cases. Access requires registration and terms; AURORA has accepted neither and accessed no payload. The challenge rules place usable data after the December 2026 embargo, later than the 26 October 2026 ISBI 2027 paper deadline. The multi-organ count also does not identify an aneurysm-specific patient subset. Public code is not present data access.

### The device phantom is interpretable but pseudo-replicated

Zenodo record `14981710` provides eight in-vitro 4D-flow MRI acquisitions across four models, two VENC settings per model, and 20 cardiac phases. Those models derive from one paraophthalmic aneurysm anatomy: untreated plus three flow-diverter configurations. This is valuable for acquisition/device sensitivity visualization, but it is not eight independent anatomies or a human outcome cohort. It cannot be the confirmatory unit for a general aneurysm paper.

## What remains valid from the supplied surface-vector analysis

The following sequence is scientifically appropriate **if** a new material version later passes source admission:

1. Pre-register a method-free stability audit for signed critical points, trajectories, and birth/death events under mesh, tolerance, and perturbation changes.
2. Demonstrate a field-error-matched structural failure in strong Cartesian, tangent-projected, equivariant, Hodge, and published operator controls.
3. Only then develop the minimum mechanism-linked model with family-disjoint splits and validation-only selection.
4. Freeze and run an untouched confirmatory family on field error, critical-point precision/recall, index error, trajectory distance, and event F1.
5. Visualize reference and controls in identical coordinates and colour scales; make no rupture-risk or clinical-utility claim without a joined outcome study.

This sequence is a re-entry contract, not the selected method. A new model name, component stack, or good MSE cannot substitute for the first two observations.
