## Bottom line

The proposed surface-vector direction should not be discarded, but the
architecture bundle in the external analysis is not a contribution. Edge
1-forms, Hodge/DEC, SE(3) message passing, periodic decoding and structural
losses are established components or controls. The unobserved claim remains:

> A field-error-matched surrogate may fail to preserve stable critical-flow
> organization over the cardiac cycle.

The Vascular Model Repository (VMR) changes the source boundary materially.
It publicly links 22 patient-specific cerebral-aneurysm entries into eleven
prespecified growing/stable pairs and advertises one time-resolved surface-VTP
CFD archive per patient. This is a genuinely new E0 source relative to the
closed AneuG job; it is not a downloader, parser or model repair.

That source change is still not evidence that critical points or tracks are
well-defined. The only admitted question is therefore narrower:

> Before fitting any surrogate or testing any growth association, are
> boundary-margin degree and secondary critical structures stable under
> registered mesh, phase, tolerance and small-field perturbations on this
> cohort?

## Direct-prior lineage and the residual gap

The obvious claims are occupied:

1. **Growth-associated scalar hemodynamics.** The VMR source paper already
   analyzes WSS, OSI and low-shear area. A later prospective 481-patient study
   directly reports size-dependent WSS mechanisms of growth
   ([DOI](https://doi.org/10.1177/0271678X251325972)).
2. **Aneurysm WSS critical points.** Existing aneurysm work extracts and tracks
   WSS critical points over the cardiac cycle and relates them to near-wall
   flow organization. Repeating this extraction is not novelty.
3. **Tracking and topology preservation.** Robust critical-point tracking and
   trajectory-preserving vector-field compression already occupy the generic
   algorithmic problem.
4. **Surface-WSS surrogates.** Equivariant transient WSS prediction and
   geometry/mesh operators already occupy field prediction. Hodge and geometric
   operator formulations are mandatory baselines.

The residual gap is not “use topology for aneurysms.” It is an evidence-order
gap: current surrogate work can report field error without first proving that
the proposed structural endpoint is numerically identifiable on the target
surface data. This gap is falsifiable. If degree, critical points or tracks are
not stable to predeclared perturbations, structure-faithful training is not a
valid next step.

## What a later paper would still have to prove

Even a P0/P1 pass would leave the paper identity inactive. A defensible ISBI
application paper would need, in order:

1. stable boundary-margin degree and secondary point/track endpoints;
2. an observed field-error-matched structural failure of strong baselines;
3. a minimal mechanism-linked intervention, not an architecture bundle;
4. family/patient-disjoint development and fresh confirmation;
5. area-weighted field non-inferiority plus structural superiority with
   patient/pair bootstrap uncertainty; and
6. same-coordinate, same-color-scale reference/baseline/proposal figures.

The 22-patient cohort is an evaluation cohort, not sufficient evidence for a
new clinical growth predictor. No claim of rupture risk or clinical utility is
permitted.
