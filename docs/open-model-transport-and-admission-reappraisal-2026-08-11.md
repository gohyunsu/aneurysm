## Decision in one paragraph

The supplied surface-vector analysis is scientifically coherent only as an
inactive, falsifiable question. It does not authorize the proposed edge-1-form,
Hodge, equivariant or temporal architecture. A second audit also finds that a
fixed public TOF-MRA model can probably be evaluated on an open external source,

occupied to form an independent paper identity. The best executable

relabelled.

## 1. What was accepted from the surface-vector proposal

The following reasoning is retained.

- Field error and stable critical-flow organization are different evaluation
  objects. A low Cartesian error does not logically imply preservation of a
  robust zero, signed degree or trajectory.
- Task stability must precede model development. Mesh, tolerance, perturbation,
  boundary margin and matching choices can manufacture or erase an apparent
  structural failure.
- A positive future result would require field non-inferiority and stable
  structural superiority over compute- and field-error-matched controls, not a
  favorable validation run.
- Patient or base-geometry family is the independent unit. Vertices, triangles,
  phases, critical points and tracks cannot inflate the sample size.

What is not accepted is equally important: edge 1-forms, Hodge decomposition,
SE(3) message passing, periodic decoding and structure losses are prior-art
components or controls. Their combination is not a contribution. Exact critical
points and worldlines remain secondary until a method-free stability audit
establishes that they are identifiable.

## 3. Why the old additive gate is insufficient

The existing eight-axis screen is useful for ordering candidates, but a simple
sum is compensatory. A candidate can receive nearly full credit for clinical
clarity, public assets, baselines, visualisation and schedule while contributing
almost no residual novelty. The concrete example below reaches 32.0 despite a
novelty score of 0.5. Passing it would reward executability rather than a
defensible paper claim.

From schema 8.8 onward, a fresh candidate is admitted only if all conditions
hold:

1. total score is at least 32.0/40;
2. residual novelty is at least 2.5/5;
3. target identifiability is at least 3.5/5;
4. asset readiness, effective independent unit and strong-baseline feasibility
   are each at least 3.0/5;
5. the residual gap is stated without relying on component stacking or naming;
6. a prospective falsifier links the proposed intervention to a documented
   failure mechanism.

This rule is **prospective only**. It does not modify historical source scores,
turn an execution-incomplete job into a failure, or retroactively close or open
an old gate. Passing it would authorize only a newly registered method-free P0,
never a model or GPU run.
