# Release-730 direct-control selection readiness

The active 584/73/73 protocol needs a release-730-specific comparison after
the Graph U-Net adapter, GHD--GPS/GINE and Transolver terminal validation
results exist. The historical comparison utility is fixed to 51 validation
cases and must not be reused.

The prepared analyzer verifies the three exact result and terminal-record
hashes, the shared 73-case validation order, private split provenance and zero
locked-test/79-extra reads. It reports every control mean, every case-paired
10,000-bootstrap contrast and the multi-endpoint Pareto set. The selected
direct control is the feasible comparator with the lowest case-mean validation
field relative L2; the registered order resolves only an exact numerical tie.

This is not a paper winner, equivalence test, candidate-selection rule or
novelty conclusion. A field interval crossing zero is inconclusive. Detailed
candidate selection is frozen separately after these direct results and before
candidate validation. The analyzer remains result-pending and non-executable
until a fresh private activation binds all six terminal artifacts.
