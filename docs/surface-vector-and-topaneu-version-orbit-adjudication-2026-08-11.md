## Executive decision

The supplied surface-vector argument is scientifically useful as a falsifiable
question, but it is not an active paper identity. Exact job
`115645.ECE-util1` ended `E`, exit 2 after 00:27:02, used no GPU, retained no
aggregate/raw PBS log/persistent probe cache and evaluated none of ten registered
scientific checks. The only valid label is **execution-incomplete / no scientific
verdict**. Its exact historical 32.0/40 source score is not repaired, rerun or
converted into evidence for or against structure-faithful WSS surrogation.

The proposed statement—field error can be small while critical-flow organization
is wrong—remains plausible and important. It is also unobserved in the available
contract. Edge 1-forms, Hodge decomposition, SE(3)-equivariant message passing,
periodic decoding, critical-point tracking and structural losses are already
direct-prior components or controls. They cannot create novelty through assembly
or naming. If a genuinely new source version later reopens this question, task
stability must precede baseline feasibility, bounded development, fresh
confirmation and external interpretation. No architecture is selected now.

A separate material source fact was found in the official TopAneu repository:
the immutable Git history preserves a 98-case batch-1 release and a later
417-scan release with documented annotation corrections. This changes the old
premise that no version orbit exists, but it does **not** clear the prospective
novelty gate. The most relevant version-aware formulation scores 31.5/40 and its
residual novelty is 2.0/5, below the mandatory 2.5 floor. The additive-score
leader reaches 32.0 only by auditing the official evaluator and has novelty
0.5/5. All six candidates are therefore rejected.

## 1. What is accepted from the supplied surface-vector analysis

### Accepted

- The scientific estimand should be structural fidelity under field-error-
  matched comparison, not another claim that a GNN lowers Cartesian MSE.
- Critical points and cardiac-cycle tracks are evaluation-first objects. They
  become losses only after mesh, tolerance and perturbation stability is shown.
- Family-disjoint patient/base-geometry splitting, validation-only selection,
  compute matching and a fresh sealed confirmation set are mandatory.
- Reference, baseline and proposal must be rendered on the same surface,
  coordinates, phase and color scale; no rupture-risk or clinical-utility claim
  follows automatically.
- `introai9` PBS is the only possible future scientific execution route.
  `junjinyong` remains completely excluded, and login-node GPU commands remain
  forbidden.

### Corrected or bounded

- “A good architecture plus performance could be enough for ISBI” is only
  conditional. A strong application paper also needs an identified endpoint,
  a directly demonstrated baseline failure and independent confirmation.
- Edge-integrated WSS is a representation choice, not a guarantee of vertex
  vectors, zeros, signed index, worldlines or birth/death events.
- Hodge components depend on the wall-patch boundary convention. They are a
  strong control, not the contribution.
- Nonzero signed total degree proves existence of at least one zero inside a
  certified region; it does not identify exact count, location or type.
- The old P0 cannot be repaired. A future source change must enter as a new
  evidence version and a new method-free gate, never as a relabelled result.

## 3. Direct-prior boundary

The residual question was red-teamed against four occupied lines:

1. Medical noisy-label learning already estimates, corrects, reweights or
   co-trains under real and synthetic annotation error.
2. Active label cleaning already shows that correcting labels affects training,
   evaluation and model selection.
3. Revised medical benchmark releases already study how curation changes
   trustworthy evaluation.
4. Hierarchical medical classification, instance-dependent noise, multi-label
   correction and fine-grained aneurysm classification/segmentation already
   occupy generic hierarchy, taxonomy and multi-task machinery.

The latest aneurysm direct prior is especially close: Shi et al. jointly perform
13-location classification, 13-location aneurysm segmentation and 13-vessel
segmentation across CTA, MRA, T2 and post-contrast T1. Therefore location × type,
vessel-aware attention, a hierarchy loss, robust loss, co-teaching or a new
acronym is not an independent contribution.

## 5. Why no P0 is registered

A method-free audit could eventually resolve same-image case lineage and
revision types, but the current scientific formulation fails the novelty floor.
Passing asset checks cannot repair that problem-level rejection. Consequently:

- no user terms acceptance is requested for this rejected version;
- no payload or annotation-content access is justified;
- no `introai9` query, PBS submission or GPU job is justified;
- no baseline, architecture, loss, paper title, contribution, table or figure is
  opened;
- jobs `115645` and `115684` remain immutable execution-incomplete histories.

The only new automation is fail-closed public metadata watch v10. It pins the
current and batch-1 Git trees, README/changelog/terms blobs and aggregate manifest
counts. A change requests a fresh human source audit only; it cannot accept terms,
download data, repair a score, register P0, select a model or authorize compute.

## 6. Re-entry condition

This TopAneu orbit may be reconsidered only if a new problem-level gap survives
the direct priors—for example, a clinically meaningful revision mechanism with
an endpoint not reducible to generic noisy-label robustness—and the fresh
non-compensatory score reaches 32 with novelty ≥2.5. At that point, and only
after explicit user acceptance of the exact data terms, a new CPU-only
method-free P0 may be prospectively registered. Its pass would authorize only a
task-adequacy P1, not a model or GPU.
