## 1. What is accepted, modified and rejected

| Proposal | Judgment | Reason |
|---|---|---|
| Field-accurate WSS predictions can lose critical-flow structure. | **Retain as a falsifiable hypothesis.** | Cartesian or area-weighted field error does not determine the number, sign or trajectory of zeros near a degenerate field. This must still be demonstrated on stable reference structures. |
| Structure-faithful transient WSS surrogation is already a new evaluation problem. | **Modify.** | Critical-point extraction, temporal tracking and aneurysm-specific WSS tracking already exist. The residual question is narrower: whether compute- and field-error-matched surrogates fail these endpoints, and whether an operator-specific representation improves them without a field-accuracy tax. |
| An edge-1-form, equivariant, Hodge model is the paper contribution. | **Reject.** | Edge-integrated forms, DEC/Hodge decomposition, equivariance and topology-aware losses are direct prior or engineering components. They become a candidate implementation only after task adequacy. |
| Good architecture and performance may support an ISBI application paper. | **Conditionally agree.** | ISBI plausibility requires stable targets, family-disjoint independent units, matched strong baselines, uncertainty over geometry-level effects and interpretable fields. A single MSE gain or three illustrative cases is insufficient. |
| The old P0 can be repaired to obtain the missing verdict. | **Reject.** | The exact contract is closed. Its absent log and aggregate cannot be reconstructed as prospective evidence. |

The strongest defensible identity, if future evidence exists, is therefore:

> **Conditional application hypothesis.** Conventional transient-WSS
> surrogates with similar field error may disagree on robust signed critical
> points and their cardiac-cycle worldlines. A structure-aware surface operator
> is useful only if it reduces this disagreement without degrading calibrated
> or deterministic field accuracy.

“Calibrated field accuracy” is allowed only for a genuinely probabilistic
output with a prospectively defined scoring rule and coverage target. A
deterministic model must report field accuracy and must not borrow calibration
language.

## 2. Candidate architecture, not selected architecture

If a materially new evidence version passes the method-free gates, the smallest
reasonable candidate is:

1. **Input:** an oriented aneurysm-wall mesh, geometric attributes and an
   observed inflow or boundary-condition waveform;
2. **field representation:** oriented edge integrals of tangent WSS as a
   discrete surface 1-form, with an explicit vertex/tangent-vector recovery
   map for evaluation;
3. **spatial operator:** an SE(3)-equivariant mesh message-passing control and a
   discrete-Hodge operator compared at matched data, parameters and compute;
4. **temporal operator:** a periodic full-cycle map over the released phases,
   with phase registration and wrap-around handled explicitly;
5. **deterministic evaluator:** triangle-interior critical points, signed
   indices, robust cross-phase matching and birth/death events.

Important cautions prevent this list from becoming decorative novelty:

- On wall patches with inlet/outlet boundaries, the Hodge decomposition and
  harmonic subspace depend on boundary conditions and mesh metric. “Harmonic”
  cannot be treated as a universal latent channel without auditing topology and
  boundary conventions.
- Edge integrals do not by themselves guarantee accurate vertex vectors or
  critical points. The reconstruction map, orientation consistency and
  discretization error require explicit controls.
- Critical points become discontinuous near degenerate zeros. Direct
  coordinate matching can be an unstable training loss; extraction and
  stability must first be established as evaluation, stratified by a local
  non-degeneracy margin.
- Eighty phases are a release convention, not a biological law. Temporal
  alignment, phase resampling and periodic closure must be evaluated rather
  than hidden inside a decoder.
- SE(3) equivariance, Hodge components, tangency and trajectory matching remain
  baselines or ablations. None is a numbered contribution alone.

## 3. Prospective evidence ladder

The following order is mandatory. No later level can compensate for an earlier
failure.

### E0 · Material source re-entry

A new surface-vector version may be registered only after an official release,
reader, manifest or independently licensed field asset materially changes the
evidence boundary. It must provide exact geometry/field correspondence,
orientation, units, phase semantics, independent geometry families and a
bounded read path. Changing transport code, adding retries or reusing the same
three objects is prohibited.

### E1 · Method-free structure stability

Before learning, extract reference critical points and tracks under frozen
mesh resolution, remeshing, tangent projection, zero tolerance, temporal
matching and small-field perturbations. Report the stable fraction of points,
signed-index conservation, trajectory reproducibility and event reproducibility
at the geometry level. Thresholds are fixed before aggregate inspection.

### E2 · Failure-mechanism and baseline feasibility

Establish that models matched within a frozen field-error tolerance can still
separate on stable structural endpoints. Required controls are Cartesian
regression, post-hoc tangent projection, an equivariant surface model, an
official or faithful GHD-family baseline, a Hodge-spectral operator, a
TRELLIS-style foundation-surface-feature control when faithfully available,
and a non-learned field perturbation floor. If field error nearly determines all
stable structural endpoints, the proposed paper identity is unsupported.

### E3 · Bounded validation development

Use family-disjoint splits and a sealed outer test. The development budget,
maximum repair rounds, parameter/compute matching, checkpoint selector and
metric matching rules are frozen first. The architecture above is one candidate
among controls; it is not privileged by its name.

### E4 · Fresh confirmatory evidence

The proposed method must be non-inferior on area-weighted field error and
consistently improve geometry-level structural endpoints. Report critical-point
precision/recall with geodesic tolerance and sign, total-index discrepancy,
trajectory assignment distance, and temporally tolerant birth/death event F1.
Confidence intervals and paired tests use geometry families—not vertices,
triangles, phases or critical points—as independent units.

### E5 · External interpretation

On a provenance-verified real-CFD or measurement asset, show reference,
baseline and proposal using the same surface, view, phase, color range and
critical-point symbols. This is physical interpretation, not evidence of
future rupture risk, treatment benefit or clinical utility.

## 4. Stop conditions

Stop this direction without architecture repair when any of the following is
observed:

- no material asset supports a fresh E0 contract before the schedule permits a
  sealed confirmatory study;
- robust points or events are too sparse or unstable for geometry-level
  evaluation;
- structural performance is effectively determined by field error after
  matching, leaving no distinct failure mechanism;
- gains disappear against either Hodge-spectral or trajectory-aware direct
  controls;
- structural gains require a material field-accuracy or calibration penalty;
- the effective independent unit is generator family rather than the reported
  number of meshes and is too small for uncertainty estimation.
